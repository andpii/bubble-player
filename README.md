# Bubble Player

A friendlier video player for **Firefox for Android**. A small bubble appears on a playing video: tap it for a
clean fullscreen player with gestures.

- **Hold** the right side of a video to play at 2× until you let go.
- **Double-tap** the sides to skip back or ahead; **swipe** sideways to seek, with a preview of the picture.
- **Swipe** up and down for volume (right) and brightness (left).

**Get it:** [Bubble Player on addons.mozilla.org](https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/bubble-player/)

## Report a problem or suggest something

This repository is for bug reports and ideas (the add-on's code isn't published here).

- [**Report a problem**](https://github.com/andpii/bubble-player/issues/new?template=bug.yml): the quickest way is
  from the add-on itself: Bubble Player settings → _Report a problem_ (it fills in the versions for you).
- [**Suggest a feature**](https://github.com/andpii/bubble-player/issues/new?template=feature.yml)
- Before reporting, have a quick look at the [open issues](https://github.com/andpii/bubble-player/issues): someone
  may have reported it already; a 👍 or a comment with your site helps too.

Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot with gesture problems.
